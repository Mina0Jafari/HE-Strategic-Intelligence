SIMULATED INSTITUTIONAL PERFORMANCE REPORT

Institution: Example Metropolitan University (Fictional)
Reporting Period: 2024/25
Dataset Version: 2

IMPORTANT:
All figures and institutional priorities in this report are fictional and created solely for testing and evaluating the HE Strategic Intelligence prototype.

The accompanying institutional_performance.xlsx workbook contains simulated school-level and programme-level performance data for the academic years 2021/22 to 2024/25.

The fictional institution uses five school codes (ACI, ACE, HSB, CSC and RDSBL) to align the demonstration dataset with the academic structure used in the research case study.

These simulated figures must not be presented as actual University of East London performance.

Publicly published University of East London annual reports and Office for Students information are separate external evidence sources.

STUDENT RETENTION

Reporting Period: 2024/25

Simulated institution-wide student retention rate: 84%.
Simulated previous-year institution-wide retention rate: 87%.
Institutional retention target: 90%.

Simulated first-year undergraduate retention rate: 79%.
Simulated postgraduate taught retention rate: 91%.

The institution-wide and student-category indicators above are independently specified synthetic values. They are not calculated from the school-level records in the accompanying Excel workbook.

The Excel workbook contains undergraduate retention rates for five schools across four academic years. School-level retention comparisons and percentage-point changes should be calculated directly from those records.

STRATEGIC PRIORITIES

The following are simulated strategic priorities for Example Metropolitan University:

1. Improve student retention to the institutional target of 90%.
2. Increase institution-wide student satisfaction to 85%.
3. Strengthen financial sustainability and monitor operating margins.
4. Improve student academic support services, particularly for groups with lower retention.
5. Expand data-informed strategic planning using institutional and programme-level performance evidence.

DATA INTERPRETATION AND LIMITATIONS

All institutional performance figures in this report are synthetic.

The Excel workbook provides more detailed simulated school-level and programme-level evidence.

Retention, continuation and completion rates describe different measures and should not be treated as interchangeable.

Institution-wide rates should not be calculated as simple averages of school-level rates without suitable population weights and consistent cohort definitions.

Observed changes or associations between retention, satisfaction, enrolment and financial measures do not establish causal relationships.

The simulated institutional dataset and actual public UEL and OfS documents must be clearly distinguished in generated reports.
